\section{Scope}
This standard defines the qualitative risk banding scheme used when assessing
consumer product safety incidents. Four bands (A through D) are defined, from
most to least severe.

\section{Banding method}
A product score is derived from two ordinal ratings supplied by the
manufacturer: the likelihood of the hazardous condition occurring and the
severity of the consequence if it does. The score is the product of the two
ratings.

\section{Band definitions}
The normative numeric cut-offs that map a product score to a band are given in
Annex B. Annex B is distributed separately and is intentionally omitted from
this source mirror. Implementations must consult the maintained reference
implementation when applying the method.
